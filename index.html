MODULATION BASICS

Carrier Signal

Carrier
\( c(t) = A_c \cos\!\left(2\pi f_c t + \theta\right) \)
Instantaneous Frequency
\( f_i(t) = \dfrac{1}{2\pi}\dfrac{d\phi(t)}{dt} \)
Instantaneous Phase
\( \phi(t) = 2\pi f_c t + \theta \)
Speed of Light / Wavelength
\( \lambda f = c = 3\times10^8 \text{ m/s} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \lambda = \dfrac{c}{f} \)

NORMAL AM — DSB-FC

Modulated Signal

General AM Signal
\( s_{AM}(t) = A_c\!\left[1 + k_a m(t)\right]\cos(2\pi f_c t) \)
Single Tone \(m(t)=A_m\cos(2\pi f_m t)\)
\( s_{AM}(t) = A_c\!\left[1 + M\cos(2\pi f_m t)\right]\cos(2\pi f_c t) \)

Modulation Index

Definition
\( M = k_a A_m \)
From Graph
\( M = \left|\dfrac{A_{max} - A_{min}}{A_{max} + A_{min}}\right| \)
Maximum Envelope
\( A_{max} = A_c(1+M) \)
Minimum Envelope
\( A_{min} = A_c(1-M) \)
M < 1 → Under-modulation (can recover)  |  M = 1 → Normal modulation  |  M > 1 → Over-modulation ❌ (cannot recover)  |  M = 0 → \(s_{AM}(t)=c(t)\), no message

Power

Carrier Power
\( P_c = \dfrac{A_c^2}{2} \)
USB / LSB Power
\( P_{USB} = P_{LSB} = \dfrac{\left(\dfrac{A_c M}{2}\right)^2}{2} = \dfrac{A_c^2 M^2}{8} \)
Total Power
\( P_{total} = \dfrac{A_c^2}{2}\!\left(1 + \dfrac{M^2}{2}\right) \)

Efficiency

Single Tone
\( \eta\% = \dfrac{M^2}{M^2 + 2} \times 100\% \)
Multi-Tone
\( \eta\% = \dfrac{M_1^2 + M_2^2 + \cdots}{2 + M_1^2 + M_2^2 + \cdots} \times 100\% \)
Max efficiency at \(M=1\): \(\eta_{max} = \frac{1}{3} = 33.3\%\)

Bandwidth

Bandwidth
\( BW = 2f_{max,\,message} \)
3

DSB-SC — Double Sideband Suppressed Carrier

Signal

DSB-SC Signal
\( s_{DSB\text{-}SC}(t) = m(t)\cdot c(t) \)
Single Tone Expanded
\( s(t) = \dfrac{A_m A_c}{2}\!\left[\cos2\pi(f_c+f_m)t + \cos2\pi(f_c-f_m)t\right] \)
USB / LSB Power
\( P_{USB} = P_{LSB} = \dfrac{A_m^2 A_c^2}{8} \)
Efficiency & BW
\( \eta = 100\%\qquad BW = 2f_m \)

Coherent Demodulation

Multiply then Filter
\( v(t) = s_{DSB}(t)\cdot c'(t)\;\xrightarrow{\text{LPF}}\; y(t)\propto m(t) \)
LPF Cutoff Condition
\( f_m \;\leq\; f_{3dB} \;<\; 2f_c - f_m \)
With Freq. Diff \(\Delta f\)
\( y(t) = \dfrac{A_m A_c A_c'}{4}\!\left[\cos2\pi(f_m+\Delta f)t + \cos2\pi(f_m-\Delta f)t\right] \)
With Phase Diff \(\Delta\theta\)
\( y(t) = \dfrac{A_c A_c'}{2}\,m(t)\cos(\Delta\theta) \)
4

SSB-SC — Single Sideband Suppressed Carrier

Phase Shift Method (Generation)

USB-SC
\( s_{USB}(t) = m(t)\,A_c\cos(2\pi f_c t) - \hat{m}(t)\,A_c\sin(2\pi f_c t) \)
LSB-SC
\( s_{LSB}(t) = m(t)\,A_c\cos(2\pi f_c t) + \hat{m}(t)\,A_c\sin(2\pi f_c t) \)
Single Tone — USB
\( s_{USB}(t) = A_m A_c\cos2\pi(f_c+f_m)t \)
Single Tone — LSB
\( s_{LSB}(t) = A_m A_c\cos2\pi(f_c-f_m)t \)
\(\hat{m}(t)\) = Hilbert transform of \(m(t)\) = 90° phase shift.
For \(m(t)=A_m\cos(2\pi f_m t)\) → \(\hat{m}(t)=A_m\sin(2\pi f_m t)\)

Power & Bandwidth

Efficiency
\( \eta = 100\% \)
Bandwidth
\( BW = f_{max,\,message} \)

Non-Coherent with Phase Diff

Output (USB-SC example)
\( y(t) = \dfrac{A_c'}{2}\!\left[m(t)\cos(\Delta\theta) \mp \hat{m}(t)\sin(\Delta\theta)\right] \)

ANGLE MODULATION — FM & PM

Frequency Modulation (FM)

Instantaneous Frequency
\( f_i(t) = f_c + k_f m(t) \)
Frequency Deviation
\( \Delta f(t) = f_i(t) - f_c = k_f m(t) \)
Peak Frequency Deviation
\( \Delta f_{max} = \left|k_f m(t)\right|_{max} \)
FM Signal
\( s_{FM}(t) = A_c\cos\!\left(2\pi f_c t + 2\pi k_f\int_0^t m(\tau)\,d\tau\right) \)
Single Tone Form
\( s_{FM}(t) = A_c\cos\!\left(2\pi f_c t + \beta\sin(2\pi f_m t)\right) \)
Modulation Index \(\beta\)
\( \beta = \dfrac{\Delta f_{max}}{f_m} = \dfrac{k_f A_m}{f_m} \)

Phase Modulation (PM)

PM Signal
\( s_{PM}(t) = A_c\cos\!\left(2\pi f_c t + k_p m(t)\right) \)
Instantaneous Freq (PM)
\( f_i(t) = f_c + \dfrac{k_p}{2\pi}\dfrac{dm(t)}{dt} \)
Peak Freq Deviation (PM)
\( \Delta f_{max} = \dfrac{k_p}{2\pi}\left|\dfrac{dm(t)}{dt}\right|_{max} \)
FM: \(f_i\) depends on amplitude of \(m(t)\) |  PM: \(f_i\) depends on slope (derivative) of \(m(t)\)

Power (FM & PM)

Total Power (always constant)
\( P_{total} = \dfrac{A_c^2}{2} \)

Wideband FM — Bessel Series

FM Signal (Bessel Expansion)
\( s_{FM}(t) = A_c\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} J_n(\beta)\cos\!\left(2\pi(f_c+nf_m)t\right) \)
Bessel Symmetry
\( J_{-n}(x) = (-1)^n J_n(x) \)
Power Property
\( \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} \bigl[J_n(x)\bigr]^2 = 1\quad\forall\, x \)
Total Power (Bessel form)
\( P_{total} = \dfrac{A_c^2}{2}\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}J_n^2(\beta) = \dfrac{A_c^2}{2} \)
Carrier Component
\( P_{carrier} = \dfrac{A_c^2}{2}J_0^2(\beta) \)

Bandwidth

99% Power BW
\( BW = 2n\cdot f_m \quad\text{(find smallest } n \text{ for 99\% power)} \)
Carson's Rule (FM & PM)
\( BW \approx 2(\beta+1)f_m = 2\Delta f_{max} + 2\,BW_{message} \)
NBFM (\(\beta\ll 1\))
\( BW = 2f_m \quad\text{(same as AM)} \)

Frequency Multiplier (Indirect FM Generation)

After Multiplier ×n
\( f_c^{new} = n\cdot f_c' \qquad \beta^{new} = n\cdot\beta' \)

SAMPLING & PAM

Nyquist Theorem

Nyquist Rate
\( f_s \;\geq\; 2\,f_{max,\,message} \)
Aliasing (if violated)
\( f_s < 2f_h \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Aliasing — cannot recover signal} \)

Sampled Signal Spectrum

General Sampler
\( M_s(f) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} f_s\,P(nf_s)\cdot M(f-nf_s) \)
Ideal Sampler \([P_n = f_s]\)
\( M_s(f) = f_s\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} M(f - nf_s) \)
Duty Cycle
\( D = \dfrac{\tau}{T_s}\times 100\% = \tau\cdot f_s\times 100\% \)
Reconstruction LPF
\( H(f)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{1}{P_0} & |f|\leq\dfrac{f_s}{2}\\[4pt]0 & \text{o.w.}\end{cases} \)

PCM — PULSE CODE MODULATION / ADC

Uniform Quantizer

Number of Levels
\( M = 2^n \)
Step Size
\( \Delta = \dfrac{A_{max}-A_{min}}{M} = \dfrac{A_{max}-A_{min}}{2^n} \)
Quantization Error
\( E = |x - \bar{x}| \;\in\; \left[-\dfrac{\Delta}{2},\,\dfrac{\Delta}{2}\right] \)
Noise Power
\( P_e = \dfrac{\Delta^2}{12} \)

SQNR — Signal-to-Quantization-Noise Ratio

SQNR (sinusoidal input)
\( SQNR = \dfrac{A^2/2}{\Delta^2/12} = \dfrac{3}{2}M^2 = \dfrac{3}{2}\cdot 2^{2n} \)
SQNR in dB
\( SQNR_{dB} = 6.02\,n + 1.76\;\text{dB} \)

Bit Rate

Bit Rate
\( R_b = n \cdot f_s \quad\text{[bits/sec]} \)

Delta Modulation — Slope Overload Condition

To Avoid Slope Overload
\( \dfrac{\Delta}{T_s} \;\geq\; \left|\dfrac{dm(t)}{dt}\right|_{max} \)
Single Tone \(m(t)=A_m\cos(2\pi f_m t)\)
\( \Delta \;\geq\; 2\pi f_m A_m T_s = \dfrac{2\pi f_m A_m}{f_s} \)

M-law (Non-Uniform Quantization)

Compressor Output
\( y = y_{max}\,\dfrac{\ln\!\left(1+\mu\dfrac{|x|}{x_{max}}\right)}{\ln(1+\mu)}\cdot\text{sgn}(x) \)

DIGITAL MODULATION — ASK / PSK / FSK

General Formulas

Symbol Duration
\( T = \dfrac{1}{R_b} \)
Avg Energy per Bit
\( E_b = \dfrac{E_1+E_2}{2} \)
Threshold (Matched Filter)
\( \lambda = \dfrac{1}{2}(E_1 - E_2) \)
Antipodal Signals \([s_1=-s_2]\)
\( \lambda = 0 \)
General Probability of Error
\( P_b = Q\!\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{\displaystyle\int_0^T[s_1(t)-s_2(t)]^2\,dt}{2N_0}}\right) \)
Matched Filter
\( h(t) = s_1(T-t) - s_2(T-t) \)

BASK — Binary Amplitude Shift Keying

Signals
\( s_1(t)=A\cos(2\pi f_c t),\quad s_2(t)=0 \quad(0\leq t\leq T) \)
Energies
\( E_1 = \dfrac{A^2T}{2}, \quad E_2 = 0, \quad E_b = \dfrac{A^2T}{4} \)
Threshold
\( \lambda = E_b \)
Probability of Error
\( P_b = Q\!\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{E_b}{N_0}}\right) = Q\!\left(\sqrt{SNR}\right) \)
BW (90% / 95%)
\( BW_{90\%}=R_b \qquad BW_{95\%}=2R_b \)

BPSK — Binary Phase Shift Keying

Signals (Antipodal)
\( s_1(t)=A\cos(2\pi f_c t),\quad s_2(t)=-A\cos(2\pi f_c t) \)
Energy
\( E_1 = E_2 = E_b = \dfrac{A^2 T}{2} \)
Threshold
\( \lambda = 0 \)
Probability of Error
\( P_b = Q\!\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{2E_b}{N_0}}\right) = Q\!\left(\sqrt{2\,SNR}\right) \)
BW (90% / 95%)
\( BW_{90\%}=R_b \qquad BW_{95\%}=2R_b \)
BPSK is 3 dB better than BASK (half the \(N_0\) needed for same \(P_b\))

BFSK — Binary Frequency Shift Keying

Signals
\( s_1(t)=A\cos(2\pi f_1 t),\quad s_2(t)=A\cos(2\pi f_2 t) \)
Frequencies
\( f_1 = f_c + \Delta f, \quad f_2 = f_c - \Delta f \)
Orthogonality Condition
\( \Delta f = \dfrac{m}{2T},\quad m\in\mathbb{Z}^+ \quad\Rightarrow\quad \int_0^T s_1 s_2\,dt = 0 \)
Energy
\( E_1 = E_2 = E_b = \dfrac{A^2 T}{2},\quad \lambda=0 \)
Probability of Error
\( P_b = Q\!\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{E_b}{N_0}}\right) \)
BW (90% / 95%)
\( BW_{90\%}=2R_b+2\Delta f \qquad BW_{95\%}=4R_b+2\Delta f \)

SNR Relationship

SNR Definition
\( SNR = \dfrac{E_b}{N_0} \)
From Pb = Q(C)
\( C = \sqrt{2\,SNR}\;\;(\text{BPSK})\quad\text{or}\quad C=\sqrt{SNR}\;\;(\text{BASK/BFSK}) \)

KEY MATHEMATICAL IDENTITIES

Trigonometric Products

cos × cos
\( \cos A\cos B = \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left[\cos(A-B)+\cos(A+B)\right] \)
sin × sin
\( \sin A\sin B = \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left[\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B)\right] \)
cos × sin
\( \cos A\sin B = \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left[\sin(A+B)-\sin(A-B)\right] \)
cos² and sin²
\( \cos^2 x = \tfrac{1}{2}+\tfrac{1}{2}\cos(2x) \qquad \sin^2 x = \tfrac{1}{2}-\tfrac{1}{2}\cos(2x) \)

Fourier Transforms

Cosine
\( \mathcal{F}\!\left\{\cos(2\pi f_0 t)\right\} = \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left[\delta(f-f_0)+\delta(f+f_0)\right] \)
Sine
\( \mathcal{F}\!\left\{\sin(2\pi f_0 t)\right\} = \dfrac{1}{2j}\!\left[\delta(f-f_0)-\delta(f+f_0)\right] \)
Sinc Function
\( \text{sinc}(x) = \dfrac{\sin(\pi x)}{\pi x} \)
Q-Function
\( Q(x) = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_x^{\infty} e^{-u^2/2}\,du \)
